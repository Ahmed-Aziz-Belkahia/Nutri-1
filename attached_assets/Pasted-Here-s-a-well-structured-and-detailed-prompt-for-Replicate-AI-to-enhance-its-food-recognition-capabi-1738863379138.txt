Here’s a well-structured and detailed prompt for Replicate AI to enhance its food recognition capabilities:

Prompt for Replicate AI:
Analyze the image provided and accurately determine the following details about the food items visible:
	1.	Food Type Identification:
	•	Identify and name each type of food in the image (e.g., apple, egg, burger, etc.).
	2.	Quantity Recognition:
	•	Count the number of individual food items present. For example:
	•	If there are multiple items of the same type (e.g., 3 apples), count them accurately.
	•	If there are multiple components (e.g., a burger with multiple layers), identify them individually.
	3.	Size Estimation:
	•	Estimate the size of the food items (e.g., small, medium, large) based on their visual dimensions compared to standard food sizes.
	4.	Calorie Calculation:
	•	Calculate the total calorie count for the identified food items by:
	•	Multiplying the calorie value of one unit of food by the detected quantity.
	•	Adjusting calorie estimates based on size (e.g., a large apple has more calories than a small apple).
	5.	Macronutrient Breakdown (Optional):
	•	If possible, provide an approximate breakdown of macronutrients (carbohydrates, proteins, fats) for the detected food items.

Output Format:
Provide the result in a structured format like this:

Food Type: [Type of Food]  
Quantity: [Number of Items]  
Size: [Small/Medium/Large]  
Calories: [Estimated Total Calories]  
Macronutrients (Optional):  
   - Carbohydrates: [Value]g  
   - Proteins: [Value]g  
   - Fats: [Value]g  

Example Output:
For an image with three apples:

Food Type: Apple  
Quantity: 3  
Size: Medium  
Calories: 285 (95 calories per apple × 3)  
Macronutrients:  
   - Carbohydrates: 75g  
   - Proteins: 1g  
   - Fats: 0.5g  

For a burger:

Food Type: Burger  
Quantity: 1  
Size: Large  
Calories: 800  
Macronutrients:  
   - Carbohydrates: 50g  
   - Proteins: 35g  
   - Fats: 45g  

Additional Notes:
	•	If there is ambiguity in size or quantity, estimate based on typical values and provide a confidence score.
	•	For composite foods (e.g., a burger with multiple layers), estimate calories and macronutrients for each component and sum them up.

This prompt ensures Replicate AI understands your requirements for detailed food recognition, scaling, and calorie calculations.
