🚀 Nutri AI: First-Time User Interactive Guide
🧭 1. Home Dashboard Overview
Objective: Familiarize users with the main dashboard.

Features Highlighted:

Daily Summary: Calorie intake, macro distribution, and meal logs.

Quick Actions: Buttons for scanning meals, adding progress photos, and checking body metrics.

User Prompt: "This is your daily hub. Here, you can track your meals, monitor your progress, and access personalized recommendations."

📸 2. Meal Scanning Feature
Objective: Introduce the AI-powered meal scanning capability.

Steps:

Tap the Scan Meal button.

Point the camera at your meal.

Confirm or adjust the detected items.

Tips:

Ensure good lighting for accurate detection.

Separate foods on the plate for better recognition.

User Prompt: "Quickly log your meals by scanning them. Our AI will identify the foods and provide nutritional information."
